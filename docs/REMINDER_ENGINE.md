# WildBear HQ Reminder Engine

## Purpose

The reminder engine exists to reduce founder drift.

The goal is not to create more notifications.
The goal is to help the founder return to leverage.

## Phase 1

Snack-safe in-app reminder cards only.

Examples:

- Post one clip today
- Move one asset forward
- Finish one revenue-producing task
- Review RPH tonight
- Advance one product by 5 percent

## Phase 2

Persistent reminder state.

Potential upgrades:

- recurring reminders
- reminder history
- streak tracking
- AI-generated reminders

## Phase 3

Native notifications after leaving Snack mode.

Potential upgrades:

- local push notifications
- daily founder briefing
- bottleneck alerts
- publishing reminders
- launch countdowns

## Product Philosophy

The reminder system should feel calm and useful.

Not noisy.
Not corporate.
Not anxiety-inducing.

The founder should feel:

"I know exactly what matters next."
